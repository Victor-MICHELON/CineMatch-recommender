# Archive

Early experiments, kept for history only. They are not maintained and are not used by the app or the pipeline.

- `hybrid-dual-tower-movie-reco-system.ipynb` — Siamese / item tower trained on MovieLens pairs (TensorFlow).
- `hybrid-movie-reco-system.ipynb` — two-tower (user × item) model with SBERT content features.

The production signals are built by [`pipeline/`](../../pipeline); the design notebook is [`../hybrid-weighted-movie-recommender-system.ipynb`](../hybrid-weighted-movie-recommender-system.ipynb).
